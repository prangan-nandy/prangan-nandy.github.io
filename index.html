About

Research at the intersection of modeling and experiment.

I am a Mechanical Engineering PhD student and Graduate Research Assistant in the RASAER Laboratory at the University of South Carolina.

My work focuses on atmospheric-pressure plasma generation, plasma-surface interactions, and plasma-assisted chemical conversion. I connect multiphysics models with synchronized electrical diagnostics, ICCD imaging, optical emission spectroscopy, and purpose-built experimental systems.

Before graduate school, I developed engineering simulation tools, supported sustainable-product data systems, and improved industrial maintenance processes through root-cause analysis and mechanical design.

Currently based in Columbia, South Carolina

Selected research

Projects built around difficult coupled-physics questions.

From piezoelectric plasma sources to sustainable energy systems, these projects combine mechanistic modeling with practical engineering.

01

NSF ECLIPSE / 2024 - Present

Unravelling the Coupled Physics of Piezoelectric Materials and Plasma Behavior

Developed a fully coupled 2D framework linking piezoelectric mechanics and plasma kinetics, then paired it with synchronized voltage-current diagnostics and time-resolved ICCD imaging to explain polarity-dependent surface-guided discharges.

02

U.S. Department of Energy / 2024 - 2025

Plasma-assisted siloxane reformation

Implemented a global plasma-chemistry model to interpret HMDSO conversion, fragmentation pathways, reactive intermediates, and power-dependent gas-phase clustering measured with molecular-beam mass spectrometry.

Global kineticsHMDSOMBMS
03

Collaborative research / 2024

Plasma-enabled lignin hydrogenolysis

Measured discharge current and plasma power to connect energy input and reaction conditions with lignin conversion and product-distribution trends in a sustainable chemical-conversion process.

Power diagnosticsBiomass conversionNonthermal plasma
04

Undergraduate thesis / 2021 - 2022

Advanced adiabatic compressed-air energy storage

Built a Python numerical model of a compressed-air energy-storage system with thermal storage and evaluated how compression staging and storage pressure affect efficiency and exergy loss.
